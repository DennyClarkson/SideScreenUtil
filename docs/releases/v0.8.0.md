# SideScreenUtil v0.8.0

## 中文

### 两个版本共同新增

- **无边框保护窗口**：在“监看”页选择窗口模式，为同一显示器内的一块区域显示屏保内容；全屏模式仍然可用，也可在运行中切换。
- **自由调整区域**：开始监看后，点击“调整窗口”或按 `Ctrl+Alt+P`，拖动窗口移动位置、拖动右下角调整大小，再次按快捷键完成调整。窗口保持在所选显示器内，位置和大小会保存。
- **鼠标移入显示桌面**：正常运行时，鼠标进入保护窗口就隐藏画面，移出后恢复；调整窗口和编辑内容布局时暂时停用隐藏。`Ctrl+Alt+L` 仍用于编辑窗口内的监看内容。
- 窗口模式保留多窗口监看、实时增删来源、滤镜、亮度、漂移、定时全黑及分辨率限制；不选来源时也能显示纯黑区域。

### 修复与改进

- 修复完整兼容版在 `PrintWindow` 后备捕获路径上可能出现的上下倒像；明确使用从上到下的图像行顺序，并规范两版 GDI 位图读取。
- 完整兼容版统一深色控件、下拉菜单、复选框、数值标签和禁用状态；设置页面可滚动，修复滑块圆柄上下被截断的问题。
- 修复完整兼容版打包时受到其他工具 DLL 搜索路径影响而无法启动的问题。
- 原生版同步提供窗口模式和中英文操作提示，继续保持单文件、约 0.6 MiB 的体积。

### 下载与升级

- `SideScreenUtil.exe`：完整兼容版（Python/Qt）。
- `SideScreenUtil-native.exe`：精简原生版（Rust/Win32）。
- 每个 EXE 都附带对应的 `.sha256` 校验文件。

退出正在运行的旧版后替换 EXE 即可。两版设置仍各自保存，旧设置可继续使用，默认保持全屏保护。程序不能保证消除 OLED 烧屏风险。

## English

### Added to both editions

- **Borderless protection window**: protect a region inside the selected display, or switch back to full-screen protection while monitoring remains active.
- Click **Position window** or press `Ctrl+Alt+P`, drag to move, resize from the bottom-right corner, then press the shortcut again to finish. Position and size are saved within the selected display.
- Entering the protected region reveals the desktop; leaving restores the canvas. Pointer reveal is suspended while positioning or editing. `Ctrl+Alt+L` continues to edit the monitored content separately.
- Window mode retains live source changes, filters, brightness, drift, black rest periods, capture-resolution options, and black-only operation with no selected sources.

### Fixed and improved

- Corrected upside-down frames in the full edition's `PrintWindow` fallback and tightened GDI bitmap readback in both editions.
- Unified the full edition's dark controls, menus, checkboxes, value labels, and disabled states. Settings pages scroll at smaller sizes, and slider handles are no longer clipped.
- Isolated Python/Windows DLL lookup during packaging to prevent incompatible DLLs from unrelated tools breaking startup.
- The native edition includes the new workflow and translated guidance while remaining a self-contained executable of approximately 0.6 MiB.

### Downloads and upgrade

Choose `SideScreenUtil.exe` (full Python/Qt edition) or `SideScreenUtil-native.exe` (compact Rust/Win32 edition). Each has a matching `.sha256` file. Exit the old application before replacing its EXE. Existing settings remain compatible and separate between editions; full-screen mode remains the default. OLED burn-in prevention cannot be guaranteed.
